## Scope and posture

This release is a visual skin over an unchanged data path. The renderers still
consume validated aggregate data only: they do not scan Git, open SQLite, infer
attribution, or recalculate statistics. `unknown` remains distinct from
human-declared activity, provider totals remain non-exclusive, and the daily
matrix keeps its existing total-commit and AI-share bin arithmetic.

The alignment rails and short section rules are guides, not marks. They encode
no metric or state, so quantitative information continues to live in labelled
text, bar height, and share fill under the existing contrast tests.

This record verifies the published artifact. It does not retag, rebuild, or
otherwise mutate anything already published under `v0.4.10`.
