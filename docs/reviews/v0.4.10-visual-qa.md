## Limitations

This is a deterministic Chromium smoke matrix, not a substitute for GitHub's
Markdown renderer or a physical-device assistive-technology audit. It reports
what a headless Chromium build renders and measures; it does not establish that
a screen-reader user, a real mobile device, or another engine reaches the same
result. The cross-platform release workflow and the maintainer Profile Pages
check have since completed, and both are recorded above and in
`docs/reviews/v0.4.10-release-readiness.md`.
